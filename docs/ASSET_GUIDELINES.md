# Asset Guidelines

## Style
- simple  
- friendly  
- symbolic  
- readable at small sizes  

## Vehicles
Six variants per tier; idle + movement animations.

## Homes
Six variants per tier; distinct silhouettes.

## Pets
Idle, hunger, passenger animations.

## Backdrops
Wallpaper + lighting + optional gags.

## Weather
Subtle overlays.

## Collectibles
Small symbolic icons.
