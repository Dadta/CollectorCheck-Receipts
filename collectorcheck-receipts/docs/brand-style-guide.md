# CollectorCheck U.S.A — Brand Style Guide

## Brand Identity
CollectorCheck U.S.A is a clean, modern ecosystem for collecting, automating, and syncing receipts across mobile, Windows, and cloud platforms.

## Core Principles
- **Clarity** — Simple workflows, minimal UI.
- **Reliability** — Stable ingestion and sync.
- **Openness** — Fully open-source, contributor-friendly.
- **Modernity** — Fresh, minimal design language.

## Voice & Tone
- Clear, confident, helpful.
- Avoid jargon.
- Emphasize simplicity and trust.

## Typography
- Inter family (Regular, Medium, Bold, ExtraBold)
- Headings: Bold or ExtraBold
- Body: Regular or Medium

## UI Guidelines
- High contrast
- Minimal shadows
- Rounded corners (4–6px)
- Consistent spacing (8px grid)

## Writing Guidelines
- Short sentences
- Clear calls to action
- Consistent terminology: “collect”, “automate”, “sync”

## Example Tagline
"Collect. Automate. Sync."

## Example Subheadline
"The clean, modern receipt workflow for freelancers, gig workers, and small businesses."
