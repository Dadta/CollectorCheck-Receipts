# What Makes CollectorCheck Receipts a New Innovation

CollectorCheck introduces a new pattern in receipt tools: treating merchants as *receipt sources* that users can connect, just like banks let users connect billers. This small shift creates a new category of receipts software.

## A New UX Pattern: Add Your Biller → Add Your Receipt Source

Banks invented “Add Your Biller” for payments.  
CollectorCheck applies the same pattern to receipts.

This is the first time users can connect:
- McDonald’s  
- Walmart  
- Amazon  
- Uber Eats  
- DoorDash  
- Shell  
- Tim Hortons  

as *receipt providers*, not just places where money is spent.

This reframes merchants as part of a receipt ecosystem.

## Why This Is New

Receipt apps today rely on:
- manual uploads  
- email forwarding  
- inbox scanning  
- camera OCR  

None of them let users add merchants directly.  
None of them treat merchants as structured receipt sources.  
None of them aggregate digital, e‑commerce, and physical receipts under one conceptual model.

CollectorCheck does.

## Why Developers Care

Developers get excited when a tool:
- introduces a new abstraction  
- defines a new category  
- applies a familiar pattern in a new domain  
- creates a clean mental model  
- is small enough to extend  

“Add Your Receipt Source” is a new abstraction layer:
- predictable  
- modular  
- easy to contribute to  
- easy to extend with merchant adapters  

This is the hook that makes CollectorCheck feel new.

## Why Users Care

Modern life generates receipts from:
- apps  
- e‑commerce  
- subscriptions  
- gig platforms  
- delivery services  
- fuel pumps  
- physical stores  

People juggle three separate receipt ecosystems:
1. app receipts  
2. e‑commerce receipts  
3. physical receipts  

CollectorCheck unifies them under one simple idea:
**connect your receipt sources.**

## Why This Matters

This pattern:
- reduces friction  
- increases trust  
- feels familiar (banking UX)  
- hides complexity  
- makes the tool feel official  
- makes the tool feel modern  

It’s a small idea with large leverage.

CollectorCheck Receipts is not just a receipts engine —  
it’s the first receipts aggregator built on a biller‑style model.
