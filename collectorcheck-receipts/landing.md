# CollectorCheck U.S.A

A simple, universal receipt collector for freelancers, gig workers, and small businesses.

## Scan. Ingest. Sync.
- Scan receipts on mobile  
- Ingest PDFs on Windows  
- Store everything securely in the cloud  

CollectorCheck U.S.A is designed to be effortless:
- No complex accounting tools  
- No bloated dashboards  
- Just clean, modern receipt organization  

## Why It Exists
People collect hundreds of receipts every year across everyday apps — drive-throughs, grocery stores, pharmacies, fuel stations, and online orders. CollectorCheck U.S.A makes it easy to keep them all in one place.

## Developer Friendly
CollectorCheck is open-source and welcomes contributors. Windows developers can help expand automation, parsing, and vendor support.

## Get Started
Download the app, scan your receipts, and keep your life organized.
